POND SITE FINDER

Where should this village build its next pond?

Upload a contour map. The terrain tells you where water already wants to collect — this finds those places, and the land draining into each one.

Accepts .kml or .kmz contour files, 1m interval or similar.

Ranked sites

Each site drains its own separate area of land — none of these catchments overlap. If the top site turns out to be unsuitable on inspection, the next one is a genuine, independent alternative, not a variation of the same spot.

Results are a preliminary planning estimate derived from the uploaded contour data — not a substitute for a field survey or civil engineering assessment.
